# kk-taste

A reference is one example from someone else's problem: weak evidence alone, and strong only when it solves the same problem and passes the bar. This skill is KK's collection: screens he believes are good and the few he rejected, his cases, and what he catches in a review, so that "good" means something concrete. The way of thinking around it is kk-1337; how a look is carried is kk-look.

## How to use it

- **The problem first.** References come after the problem and its core idea are agreed with the owner (kk-1337); a reference brought earlier pulls the answer toward its own problem.
- **Scan the whole index; it is short.** Match by the problem, in any domain: `problem/`, `idea/` and `pattern/` first, `kind/` last. The owner's own index, when their notes name one, is scanned first and wins where the two disagree.
- **Open the shots**, in `shots/` in this skill's folder. A tag is a claim to check against the shot, not a description.
- **Bring them as questions:** what this one does, what you would take from it, and whether the owner wants its look, its thinking, or neither. Take the thinking, never the surface or the signature.
- **Nothing good enough?** Search together, and hold every find to the bar at the end: most design doesn't pass it.
- **Add to the owner's index** what they approve, with their words, when they keep one; this one is KK's.

**Tags.** `problem/` the problem it solves, in words any domain shares · `kind/` what it is · `platform/` · `idea/` the one thing it proves · `look/` the contrasts that carry it · `mood/` what it feels like · `pattern/` a reusable form.

## KK's bar, in his words

- "Bold, clean, swiss and stylish thoughtful design with parts that are truly beautiful and unique."
- "grotesk or not grotesk depends on the style. So we need to identify the soul and use the contrasts."
- On sources: "most of the design is shit"; he wants "very hard standards to what we will take as reference".
- On motion: "All beautifully animated." "Beautifully means intuitively, simple, and elegant."
- On the magic: "the best and the most magic way — when the systematics aligns with the nature of task and surface." "It's special not because we invented new. It's special because nobody on earth come up with such simplicity."
