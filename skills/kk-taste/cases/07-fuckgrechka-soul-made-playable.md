# fuckgrechka: the soul made playable

**Question/task.** KK: "This is one of the best examples of design we have so far." It is his own studio's site for two products: Тяжеловато ("a bit tight", a budget calculator, free for ten years) and Койн ("Coin", paid help with your work, based on what the market does). What makes it the bar, in the skill's terms?

**Gist.** Every layer comes from one soul: friends who help you out of a mess, with a filthy sense of humour and honest numbers. The name, the line and the gesture are one idea. "Гречка" is buckwheat, the cheap food of hard times; the line «разгребаем пиздец по зёрнышку» ("we rake through the mess, grain by grain") plays on the proverb about the hen that pecks grain by grain; and on the home page, 3D buckwheat grains, money stacks and coins cover the text until you rake them aside with the cursor. Around that one playful element the rest is strict: one grotesk in one weight, black and white, colour only in the objects, and headlines that tell a story.

## The principles, as they appear here

**The soul decides everything, including the name.** The studio's name, line, pictures and gesture say the same thing. Тяжеловато's own copy speaks in that voice: «По умолчанию живём на прожиточный минимум» ("By default we live on the subsistence minimum"), and at zero «Денег на сегодня больше нет. Надеюсь, это было что-то важное» ("No money left for today. I hope it was something important"). Humour carries the soul; the numbers carry the trust.

**Two or three strong contrasts, the rest quiet.** Size (a statement of four huge lines against 18 px facts), tone (full white against half white; black against half black), texture (flat type against 3D objects and photos), and between the two products, night against day. There is almost nothing else: no boxes around text, no icons in circles, no gradients in the interface.

**Type: one family, one weight.** PP Neue Montreal Bold everywhere. The hierarchy comes from three sizes (about 66, 27 and 18 px at 1440) and two tones, not from weights or faces. It is the plainest Swiss system, and it reads bold because nothing competes with the size contrast.

**Colour lives in the objects, not in the interface.** The interface is black, white and grey. The orange wordmark on the studio page and the products' own icons (a yellow ↵ key, a yellow winking coin) are the only accents; the colour of the page comes from the 3D things and the photos, which carry the colours of the real world (the banks' own brand colours on their toy icons).

**Headlines tell the story; each section has three tiers.**
- The headlines are verbs in the first person plural and read as a story: Тяжеловато "Контролируем бюджет в простом калькуляторе → Считаем бюджет → Записываем по ходу → Радуемся накоплениям → Бесплатно навсегда" (control the budget → count it → write down as you go → enjoy the savings → free forever). Койн "Развиваем работу с опорой на рынок → Сверяемся с рынком → Разбираемся вместе → Знакомимся по делу → Пробуем бесплатно".
- Under each headline, two paragraphs that open with a bold white statement and go on in grey: «**Сумма на период.** Укажите деньги до следующего дохода — получите бюджет на каждый день.» Reading only the bold leads gives the whole page in a minute.
- Then two small facts side by side ("Любая валюта", "Перерасход"): the detail for whoever wants it.
Three depths of one story, set by type alone, with nothing to open or click.

**Data on the page and on the controls.** «4,7 ★ · 1000+ оценок», «10 лет бесплатно», «158K примеров из менторских встреч со 100+ специалистами», «103 участников · 270 000 ₽ средний доход · 900 000 ₽ максимальный доход», «400+ групповых разборов», «От 1337 ₽/мес.» (a leet joke in the price, my reading). Buttons carry their condition under the label: «Вести бюджет / iOS, Android и веб», «Попробовать / Аудит стратегии бесплатно». Times are converted for the reader: «Будни · 15:30 у вас» ("Weekdays · 15:30 your time").

**Honesty as structure.** The last card answers the question a free product raises: «Откуда деньги. Мы развиваем Тяжеловато за счёт платного Койна» ("Where the money comes from. We fund Тяжеловато with paid Койн"). Both products are then described in the same four lines in parallel («…10 лет помогает контролировать бюджет. Бесплатно.» / «Койн помогает развивать работу с опорой на рынок. За деньги.»), and the card is in the other product's colours, so it is also the way across: the dark page ends in a light card, the light page in a dark one.

**Navigation made of the products themselves.** Top left, the two app icons: the current product large, the other small. There is no menu bar.

**The touch era, on the web.** Big type, big buttons with a second line, the real app in a real hand, and on a phone the pictures fall into the flow under each section.

## Motion and visuals
- **The studio page:** the text arrives first, clean, so the first second is the offer. About a second later, low-poly 3D buckwheat grains, dollar stacks and coins tumble in from the edges, drift over the page, and roll away from the cursor. On a phone they fall and pile at the bottom of the screen. The show is the tagline itself, and it works: it is the most memorable thing on the site, it costs nothing to understand, and it waits until the offer has been read.
- **The fixed picture on the product pages:** at each section the old picture blurs and fades out (opacity and a move over 800 ms on `cubic-bezier(.33, 1, .68, 1)`, a blur over 400 ms) and the new one grows in (500 ms), its parts one after another (delays of 150 and 200 ms, 350 ms each). System-started motion with no overshoot, coordinated, one direction: motion.md by the book.
- **Койн's market section:** the plush icons of real companies fly past the camera, huge and out of focus, then clear away and leave a black card of real incomes. A show moment that means something: the market, made of the companies people work at.
- **Visuals with a job:** a hand in warm light using the real app (Тяжеловато: the product at night, when people count money); plush toys and memoji people (Койн: friendly, not corporate); a 2016 photo of someone eating from a noodle box, struck through with one orange line, with the press logos pinned to it (my reading: the broke years, crossed out with one stroke).

## What a review would still flag (the site is in progress)
- **The fixed icons land on the text.** The product switcher sits over the text column: on /tzlvt and /coin at 1440 it covers the headlines as they scroll under it ("по ходу", "вместе"), and on a phone it covers the first words of every section. A fixed layer needs a lane of its own, or it must step aside over text.
- **Once the grains arrive, they lie on the text until someone rakes them.** That is the joke, and it works for people who play. It fails for anyone who asked for less motion: with reduced motion the grains still arrive and cover the headline and the products. The flip: with `prefers-reduced-motion`, the pile rests beside the text, or the grains stay out.
- **Grey that means "the other product" is too faint.** In the final cards the dimmed description of the other product is #ababab on #f4f4f4 (2.1:1) on /tzlvt, and grey detail on /coin is 3.9:1. The dimming is a signal, so keep the difference and raise the contrast (rubric A2).
- The audit of /coin counts ten font sizes, but most belong to the pictures (the chat screen in the phone, the income card). The page's own type has three to four sizes: scope the audit before counting.
