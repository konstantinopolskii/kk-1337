# What KK sees

**Gist.** Ten things KK catches again and again when he looks at a design, distilled from about 340 of his comments across 13 projects (Jun–Sep 2026). Check each before he sees anything. Most often he catches the words, the layout to the pixel, noise without a job, one system, and the soul of the look; the costliest misses are a part designed without the whole task and a screen drawn instead of the whole job.

His comments stay with him, verbatim; here the counts say how many of the 295 comments from the session logs carry each theme, and one comment can carry two or three. His words are verbatim; Russian has an English gloss in brackets. Projects that are not his own are named by their kind.

## The self-check

### 1. The whole task, and only it

It was agreed as a whole before anything was drawn, including what the part he pointed at belongs to. Only what he asked for changes; approved work stays exactly as it was. When he gave an idea, it is drawn as he said it. (Aligning first: 28; keeping what was good: 18.)

- "You also didn't aligned with me on the whole task and didn't really get that I was also talking about the way we filter the tasks at the background." (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "Последнюю страницу с оплатой ты переделал коряво. Я попросил добавить только лид. А ты зачем-то всё остальное там тоже переделал." [You redid the payment page clumsily. I only asked to add the lead. You went and redid everything else there too.] (a tenders report, 21 Sep)
- "Why did you delete the 3d transformation from companies and logotypes widgets? I din't ask for it." (Jazzylea, 20 Jun)
- "This is almost the idea that I was sharing with you, but less elegant." (Horizons, 28 Sep)

### 2. The whole job, every click

It carries the person from the moment the need arises to the moment it is resolved. Every step and every control is answered: what the next frame is, what a click on "Other" does. The motion between the steps shows how it works. A clearer view of the problem is not yet a solution. (Flows: 13; motion: 17; the whole job: 4; and most of round four.)

- "A good solution for the problem solves it explicitelly. … But how exactly the interraction would look like? How it changes the way we move this? No answers." (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "again haf baked: what if I click other?" (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "you basically should see like a "rectangle shadow" where this will be dropped" (a client project, 9 Aug, on dragging cards)
- "Same time we should show how it all works with an elegant animation, so it simple and intuitive." (Horizons, 28 Sep)

### 3. One form, one meaning

Each thing keeps the meaning its kind has in the product and does what its form promises. One concept has one name and one mechanism; no parallel mechanism or new term appears for something that already exists. (29.)

- "why the fuck it's a almost horizon but not concept, logically blurring the whole columns in horizons idea it's like mixing bananas and dust." (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "НЕТ НИКАКОГО БАНКА ВОПРОСОВ. ЕСТЬ ПРОФИЛЬ СИТО И ОТКРЫТЫЕ ВОПРОСЫ ВНУТРИ НЕГО" [THERE IS NO QUESTION BANK. THERE'S A PROFILE SIEVE AND OPEN QUESTIONS INSIDE IT] (fuckgrechka, 22 Jul)
- "Sorry, but why do we have different values on one page. Live music ≠ Music performances." (Jazzylea, 16 Sep)
- "Right now click on the video opens a photo. That's stupid." (Jazzylea, 20 Jun)

### 4. One system

One rule or mechanism serves every case, and a fix lands at the component level. The product's own design system and components are reused, the same way everywhere, never a look-alike. (Systematic: 42; consistency: 38.)

- "We search for simple solutions, I would say systematic and universal ones. … it grows exponentially with different types of tasks and sessions." (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "I didn't get the part where you said that you needed to configure something additionally to make the fade — like switches or something, I don't like that it's not systematic." (an animation editor, 24 Sep)
- "We don't have such buttons anywhere. Rounding is wrong here. Check our components." (Jazzylea, 20 Jun)
- "Why do I see the shitty slop web pages instead of the kk design system based web pages?" (a client project, 21 Jul)

### 5. Magic

The simplest direct form, made of what is already there: intuitive, simple and elegant. It is special through simplicity, not invention. (16, and round four.)

- "It doesn't feel magical." (Horizons, 26 Sep)
- "You can open each level and then dive deeprt and deeper, while the rest is still acessible. THis is the true magic." (an animation editor, 25 Sep)
- "It's special not because we invented new. It's special because nobody on earth come up with such simplicity." (Horizons, chat, 28 Sep)
- "Beautifully means intuitively, simple, and elegant." (Horizons, 28 Sep)

### 6. Nothing without a job

No second copy of what is already there, no option for a scenario that is not this one, no line, arrow, border, dot, blur or caption that carries nothing. The most important thing is the biggest, and the rare lives one step away. (Clean: 44; redundant: 20; weight: 13; overloaded: 7; frequency: 4.)

- "Calendar is crazy overwhelmed and proposes ALL fucking options for ALL scenarios, which doesn't fucking make sense." (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "Some of the bullets are redundant. Like details. Why the fuck I need this if I can simply click?" (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "Ну неужели не ясно, что всё должно быть только про первый тендер. Всё остальное в этой ситуации мусор" [Isn't it obvious everything should be about the first tender only? Everything else here is trash] (a tenders report, 25 Sep)
- "Didn't I ask not to use fucking arrows?" (an animation editor, 25 Sep)
- "Or no. Red is red. This color is redundant. Kill." (design-system, distilled kit palette, 29 Sep)

### 7. The words

A first-time reader gets it in a glance, from the words alone. The words are plain, exact and fresh, stated rather than hedged, with facts rather than adjectives; no jargon, newspeak, translated phrasing or filler; the term already agreed, not a new one. His meaning survives every rewrite. (Words: 64; understood at a glance: 17.)

- "human that reads it for the first time should understand key values … in fraction of a second. Without us pointing them to. Simply because of the way we choose words." (Jazzylea, 16 Sep)
- "И не надо «Предлагаю» Надо просто декларировать." [And no "I propose": just state it.] (a tenders service, 16 Sep)
- "Мы говорим с людьми на простом языке, им как будто 15 лет, скажем так." [We talk to people in simple language, as if they were 15, let's say.] (fuckgrechka, 19 Jul)
- "Ты потерял же смысл из моего текста который я прислал." [You lost the meaning of the text I sent you.] (a tenders service, 16 Sep)

### 8. The layout, to the pixel

The longest real data fits. What wraps where is decided; sizes agree across the whole thing, levels contrast clearly, things align on the right line, and an anchor object is never split. It reads on a phone first. The rules are in kk-typography. (62.)

- "didn't u see that you have a long name and it doesn't fit the line? Didn't u double-check yourself with the rules of layout?" (Horizons, 28 Sep)
- "the exact contrast between text sizs, spacing betweel elements is wrong" (Jazzylea, 24 Sep)
- "Бирка с цифрой должна быть в 3 раза крупнее, потому что отчёт в первую очередь открывают с телефона." [The number tag should be 3× bigger, because the report is opened on a phone first.] (a tenders report, 25 Sep)
- "Не разрывай никогда якорные обекты лёгкими текстами." [Never break anchor objects apart with light text.] (a tenders report, 25 Sep)
- "Layout looks fucked up, hope it’s just quick draft" (design-system, distilled kit palette, 29 Sep)

### 9. The soul

One look, from the product's soul, with one part that is truly beautiful. It is never generic output, and never generic in one half and good in the other. The impression comes from knowing exactly what to add, not from decoration. (43.)

- "Bold, clean, swiss and stylish thoughtful design with parts that are truly beautiful and unique." (kk-design skill, 25 Sep)
- "Это не рюшками достигается, а в чётком понимании того, что именно надо на него добавить" [That isn't achieved with frills, but with a clear understanding of exactly what to add] (a tenders service, 14 Sep)
- "Also it feels generic at the first half and then suddenly becomes cool at the second half. It feels wrong." (Jazzylea, 16 Sep)
- "Стилистика с красным цветом и капс НЕ НАША" [The red-and-caps style is NOT ours] (a tenders report, 25 Sep)
- "It all looks right in terms of placement and positioning. But it doesn't look as a polished apple like product… Cluttery, noisy, dirty shadows, not fluffy and clean at all." (Horizons, 29 Sep)
- "But should be more like an example, but rule should be abstract, so model wouldn’t try to do exactly same. The thing is to find unique soul, not to spawn 3d everywhere." (design-system, distilled kit objects, 29 Sep)
- "Did you understand what I expect at the end for the mockups? RIght now you are making the mockups very shitty — they don't look at the end same stylish as the ones before." (design-system, distilled kit round 2, 29 Sep)

### 10. Checked, not assumed

It was compared with the original or the reference by a method (an overlay diff, a blur, a recording of the flow), tried by hand at the sizes he will use, and looked at with fresh eyes before he saw it. The check is yours: in July and August he asked for other agents to verify, and on 28 Sep he set the rule that no agents critique each other, because he is the critic. (33.)

- "Did you compare each component one by one to exclude difference in the design? Did you compare animation." (Jazzylea, 15 Jun)
- "И даже на телефоне сам ты не протестировал хотя бы в браузере свои свайпы, никаких E2E тестов." [You didn't even test your own swipes on a phone, or at least in a browser. No E2E tests.] (a food app, 1 Aug)
- "посмотри ты свежим взглядом на свою работу дизайнерским. Где твой стив джобс. Где noise ink ratio от тафти." [Look at your own work with a designer's fresh eyes. Where's your Steve Jobs? Where's Tufte's noise-ink ratio?] (a tenders report, 25 Sep)
- "if you will blur the page you will see that it's not helping eye to scan." (a tenders report, 26 Sep)

## Keeping it current

After each round, his comments are read against this list: a comment that fits a point adds to its evidence, and one that fits none is either a new point or a sign that a point is worded wrong. kk-1337's "Check it as the owner will" changes the same way, so the two stay one list.
